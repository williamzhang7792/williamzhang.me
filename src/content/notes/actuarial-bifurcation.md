The short version of where I'm going:

The classical track codifies what the profession has always done: exams, reserving, pricing within a known regulatory frame.
The integrated track meets modern technology head-on: programmable infrastructure, AI risk, MGAs that look more like software companies than carriers.
The interesting questions are at the intersection: methodology that's old enough to be rigorous, applied to risks new enough that the existing toolkit only half-applies.
