The thesis I want to develop:

Modern MGAs (Cowbell, Coalition, Vouch, others) aren't just "carriers with a tech sticker." They're structurally different — programmatic underwriting, continuous monitoring, tighter feedback loops between data and price. That changes what actuarial work *looks like* on the inside.

A classical actuary at a carrier writes a memo. An actuary at a modern MGA writes code that *is* the memo. Same rigor, different artifact, different velocity.
