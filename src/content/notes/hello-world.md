This is the first piece in what I hope becomes a real notebook — a place to put thinking that's developed enough to write down but not finished enough for anywhere else.

I'm planning to write about three things, mostly. The first is the bifurcation of actuarial work — the way the profession seems to be splitting into a classical track that codifies what it has always done and an integrated track that meets modern technology head-on. I've been thinking about which path makes sense, why, and what the integrated path actually looks like in practice.

The second is emerging risk — particularly the methodology questions around AI agent liability insurance, where the data is sparse, the failure modes are novel, and the existing actuarial toolkit only half-applies. There's a lot of writing about whether AI risk is insurable; less about how you'd actually price it.

The third is whatever I happen to be reading. I don't promise these will be useful to anyone but me. They might be the most interesting ones.

If you found this and it sat unchanged for months, the rest of the site is the more honest record of what I'm doing. The notebook is for the half-formed thoughts.
